CINEMATIC NIGHT LIGHTING:
Warm white streetlights create natural highlights on the black metallic bodywork. Blue and purple illuminated skyscrapers create subtle colored reflections on the doors and hood. Red LED taillights produce realistic reflections on slightly wet asphalt. White headlights create controlled illumination.
Maintain realistic exposure, visible black-paint surface detail, balanced highlights, natural shadows, restrained neon saturation, and physically plausible reflections. Avoid excessive glow, blown-out headlights, and artificial light beams.
